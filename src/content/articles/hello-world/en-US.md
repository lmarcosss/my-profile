Writing is one of the best ways to solidify what you learn.

In this portfolio, each article is a Markdown file. The page renders the title, the cover image, and this Markdown body — nothing fancy, just content that is easy to update whenever an idea shows up.

I like keeping notes about bugs I fixed, trade-offs I made, and small patterns that worked well in real projects. Those notes tend to turn into posts like this one.

A few guidelines I try to follow:

- Prefer short sections over long walls of text
- Show the idea with a concrete example when possible
- Leave room to revise later without rewriting everything

If you are reading this, the articles feature is working. More posts will land here as I write them.
